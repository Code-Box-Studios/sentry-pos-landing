# Cashier guide

[Guide home](README.md) · [Accounts and access](accounts-and-access.md) · [POS terminal guide](pos-terminal.md) · [Manager guide](manager.md)

A cashier works at POS terminals in branches assigned by the business owner. You use your own name and personal six-digit PIN. Your sales and drawer activity are recorded under your identity.

Open the [POS terminal](https://sentry-pos-terminal.onrender.com/). Cashiers do not sign in to the management portal, even when the owner has recorded an email address for them.

## Start using your cashier account

1. Ask the business owner to add you as a **Cashier** and assign the branches where you work. An email address is optional.
2. Receive your six-digit **temporary PIN** privately from the owner. It is displayed once on the owner’s screen after creation or reset. Cashier setup does not depend on email delivery.
3. Go to an assigned, paired terminal. Check the branch shown on **Unlock this terminal**.
4. Choose your own name.
5. Enter **Temporary PIN**.
6. Choose a different six-digit **New terminal PIN** and enter it again in **Confirm terminal PIN**.
7. Select **Set PIN and unlock**. This activates your access and unlocks the terminal.
8. Follow the [POS terminal guide](pos-terminal.md) to open your own shift and serve your first customer.

Temporary PINs expire after 15 minutes, work once, and stop working after five incorrect attempts. A new PIN from the owner replaces the previous one. If setup fails, ask the owner for **Issue fresh setup PIN** or a terminal PIN reset.

If the terminal already has an open drawer for another person, that person must close their shift before you start. Choose your own name for every shift and use only your own personal PIN.

## Unlock, lock and hand over the terminal

### Unlock for your next shift or return from a break

1. Open the [POS terminal](https://sentry-pos-terminal.onrender.com/) on the paired device.
2. Check the branch, choose your name, enter **Your PIN**, and select **Unlock terminal**.
3. If you have an open drawer, the terminal resumes it. If no drawer is open, open a shift as described in the terminal guide.
4. If an unresolved payment appears, resolve it before starting another sale, using the terminal guide.

### Lock when stepping away

1. Select **Lock** in the top bar.
2. On return, choose your name and unlock with your personal PIN.

Locking keeps the cart, unresolved payment and drawer intact. Reloading the page requires your PIN again. The terminal also locks after five minutes without user input and requires a fresh unlock after a 12-hour operator session.

### Hand over to another cashier

1. Finish or resolve the current sale/payment.
2. Close your own shift and count the drawer using the [shift-closing procedure](pos-terminal.md).
3. Lock the terminal.
4. The next operator chooses their own name, enters their PIN and opens a new shift.

An open drawer belongs to the person who opened it. Another cashier, manager or owner cannot simply unlock as themselves to take it over. A manager’s approval of an action also leaves the drawer under your name. If the original operator’s access has been removed, contact the manager or owner for the terminal guide’s emergency-close procedure.

## Follow the daily cashier workflow

Use the [POS terminal guide](pos-terminal.md) for the detailed screen-by-screen procedures.

| Point in your day | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Unlock under your name, check the branch/terminal, count opening cash and open your shift. |
| Build a sale | Add products or scan barcodes; choose variants/modifiers and quantities; check the order type; use eligible configured discounts or SC/PWD handling. |
| Pause a customer order | Hold the cart and resume it using the terminal guide. |
| Take payment | Check totals and payment method, complete payment, then print the receipt. Preserve and resolve any payment whose result is uncertain. |
| Find a past sale | Open **History**, select the date and sale, and follow the receipt/reprint or correction procedure. |
| Record drawer movements | Use the shift cash movement procedure with the amount, type and explanation. |
| Correct a sale or stock count | Request manager/owner approval when the terminal requires it. |
| Finish | Close your own shift, count cash, review/print the closing report and lock the terminal. |

## Get approval for a sensitive action

A cashier needs an active manager assigned to this branch, or the business owner, to approve **voids**, **refunds** and **terminal stock adjustments**.

1. Prepare the action in the terminal, including the required reason or corrected quantity.
2. Ask the manager or owner to review it in person.
3. In **Manager or owner approval**, the approver selects their own name under **Approver**.
4. The approver enters their own six-digit **Approver PIN**.
5. Complete the action with its confirmation button and check the result.

The approval covers that action only. Your drawer stays under your name. Your own PIN or another cashier’s PIN cannot substitute for an eligible approver. Normal sale/shift rules still apply; for example, a void must be eligible under the terminal guide’s rules.

If no approver appears, ask the owner to check the manager’s active status, completed setup and branch assignment.

## Know what your role can do

| Task | Cashier access |
| --- | --- |
| Sell products, hold/resume carts and take supported payments | Allowed during your own open shift. |
| Apply discounts configured by the owner and eligible SC/PWD treatment | Allowed where available in the sale screen. |
| Add a miscellaneous/open-price item | Allowed when enabled for the business. |
| Enter your own free-form discount amount or percentage | Unavailable to cashiers and managers. Ask the owner to configure the required discount. |
| Print or reprint receipts | Allowed; follow the terminal guide. |
| Open and close a shift, record cash movements | Allowed for your own drawer. |
| Void/refund a sale or adjust terminal stock | Assigned manager/owner approval required. |
| Receive a delivery | Ask the owner/manager to receive it in portal Stock; the terminal only has stock adjustment. |
| View **My activity** | Your sales and drawers in the paired branch. |
| View **History** | Receipts recorded at this terminal; this is separate from My activity. |
| Receipt paper width, preview and test print | Available in terminal **Settings**. |
| Pair a device or confirm terminal **Unpair…** | Requires owner sign-in. |
| Portal reports, stock transfers/counts, catalog/prices, staff or business settings | Owner/manager portal work within their permissions; unavailable to cashiers. |

The business owner controls your role, assigned branches and status. **Pending** means setup or a credential reset is unfinished. **Active** means access is ready. **Deactivated** means access has been removed.

## Review your activity

1. Select **My activity** in the POS top bar.
2. Review your **Sales** and **Drawers** for this branch. The page shows the latest 100 of each.
3. Drawer over/short variance appears after the drawer closes.
4. Select **Refresh** to reload the information.

Use **History** for receipt lookup and reprinting. History lists sales at the current terminal, so it can include another operator’s earlier receipts. My activity is the view filtered to your own identity.

## Change or recover your PIN

1. Ask the business owner to use **Reset terminal PIN** on your staff record. Close your own shift before the reset when possible.
2. Receive the new temporary PIN privately from the owner. Existing sessions end, and your account becomes **pending**.
3. At an assigned terminal, choose your name.
4. Enter **Temporary PIN**, a different **New terminal PIN**, and **Confirm terminal PIN**.
5. Select **Set PIN and unlock**.

If the owner has hidden or lost the displayed temporary PIN, they must issue a fresh one. The previous PIN cannot be displayed again.

Role or branch changes can end your current session. A change of role requires fresh setup. Reactivation can restore your existing complete PIN; if setup is incomplete, the owner must provide a new temporary PIN. Ask the owner which path applies.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your name is missing | Check the terminal’s branch. Ask the owner to verify your assignment and status. |
| The terminal shows **Pair** instead of your names | Ask the owner to pair the device. Cashier credentials cannot pair it. |
| Temporary PIN rejected | Use the newest PIN and check your name. It may be expired, used, replaced or exhausted after five wrong attempts. Ask the owner for a fresh one. |
| Your personal PIN is locked | Four failed attempts trigger a five-minute lock. Wait for the displayed retry period, then enter the correct PIN. Ask the owner for a reset if you do not know it. |
| Only another operator can resume the drawer | That person must close their shift. Contact the manager/owner if the operator’s access has been removed. |
| Approval PIN is rejected | Ask the approver to check their identity/PIN and assignment. An approver’s PIN lockout can also prevent approval. |
| The screen returns to unlock | Unlock again with your own PIN. If denied, ask the owner whether your access, role, email or branches changed. |
| A discount or option is missing | Ask the owner to check the business configuration. Cashiers cannot enter free-form discounts or change the catalog. |
| A payment or operation’s result is unclear | Keep the original transaction and follow the terminal guide’s recovery steps. Ask the manager before creating another sale or repeating the operation. |
| Network/API unreachable | Check the connection and inform the manager. Follow the terminal guide’s guidance; online selling requires connectivity. |

Give your manager or owner the branch, terminal code, action, approximate time and displayed error/reference. Do not send passwords or PINs.
